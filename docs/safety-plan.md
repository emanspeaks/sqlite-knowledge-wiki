# Software safety plan

The software safety effort, when the project has safety-critical software (section 4.2 of NASA-STD-8739.8B; handbook topics 5.18 and 8.51), in the [assurance plan](sap.md) or here.

## Safety-critical components

The components the hazard analyses make safety-critical, by the criteria of section 4.2, and the extent of the safety effort for each.

## Processes and analyses

The hazard analysis and safety assessments, their schedule, and the safety deliverables, coordinated with the system safety panel; see [hazards](hazards.md).

## Organization, resources and communication

Who does what, the people and tools, and how results reach the project and safety.

## Training and records

Safety-specific training; where the safety products are kept.
