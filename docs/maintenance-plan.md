# Software maintenance plan

Operations, maintenance and retirement of the software (%[SWE-075], %[SWE-195], %[SWE-196]), after handbook topic 5.04.

## Process

How problems and modifications are analyzed, implemented, reviewed and accepted; migration; retirement and archiving.

## Assurance and risk

Software assurance in maintenance, and the risk assessment of every change.

## Upgrades and delivery

Upgrade intervals and their tracking; the equipment and labs to verify changes; how modified software is tested before use, delivered and installed; licensing; operational backups.
