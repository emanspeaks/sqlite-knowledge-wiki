# Cost estimate and schedule

The software cost estimates (%[SWE-015], %[SWE-151], %[SWE-174]) and schedule (%[SWE-016], %[SWE-018]).

## Cost estimate

The models and parameters used (two models for classes A and B above the threshold of %[SWE-015]), the estimate and its assumptions, and when it is redone.

## Schedule

The software schedule, its milestones and reviews, coordinated with the project's, and the suppliers' schedules (%[SWE-046]).
