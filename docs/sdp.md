# Software development and management plan

The plans of the software (%[SWE-013]), started as soon as the scope is known and complete by the end of requirements analysis (handbook topic 5.08). May hold the configuration management, assurance and maintenance plans of a small project.

## Organization and authority

The organizations, including safety and mission assurance, IV&V and suppliers, and who decides what.

## Classification and tailoring

The class and safety-criticality of each system with software ([classification](classification.md)), and the tailored [Requirements Mapping Matrix](rmm.md) for Technical Authority approval.

## Engineering environment

Development, test and maintenance environments: libraries, equipment, facilities, standards and tools.

## Work breakdown, cost and schedule

The products and services, budgets, staffing and acquisition approach (%[SWE-015], %[SWE-016], %[SWE-033]); how actuals are tracked against the plans (%[SWE-024]).

## Life cycle and milestones

The life cycle model, the milestones and reviews at which progress is reviewed (%[SWE-037]), and hardware and software integration, delivery and maintenance.

## Verification and validation

Methods and criteria across the life cycle (peer reviews, testing, analysis, demonstration, prototyping, acceptance testing), the products verified or validated, their environments, and where records and corrective actions are kept.

## Quality, safety, security and privacy

How quality characteristics and critical requirements are managed; the security plan (%[SWE-156]); off-the-shelf, reused and open source components (%[SWE-027]).

## Suppliers

Insight into suppliers, access to their products and source code (%[SWE-039], %[SWE-040], %[SWE-042]).

## Configuration management, peer reviews and metrics

How each is done, or where its plan is: [configuration management](scmp.md), [peer reviews](peer-reviews.md), [metrics](metrics.md).

## Risk management and training

How software risks are managed ([risks](risks.md)) and the training the project needs (%[SWE-017]).
