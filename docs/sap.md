# Software assurance plan

The plan of software assurance and software safety for the project (%[SWE-022]), after handbook topics 5.17 and 8.51, written with the safety and mission assurance organization.

## Activities and methods

Planning and oversight across the life cycle: audits, assessments, status reporting, analyses; the reviews supported, test witnessing, and the analysis methods (hazard analysis, FMEA, FTA, static analysis).

## Roles, resources and organization

Who does what of NASA-STD-8739.8, the people, tools and access.

## Safety-criticality and classification

The independent assessment of, or concurrence with, the [classification](classification.md).

## Software assurance requirements mapping matrix

The tasks of NASA-STD-8739.8 the project's assurance carries out, with their tailoring.

## Metrics, issues and reporting

The metrics kept (at least those of NASA-STD-8739.8), how issues are tracked, and the status reports.

## Software safety

When the software is safety-critical: its components, the hazard analyses and their schedule, and the safety deliverables (handbook topic 5.18).
