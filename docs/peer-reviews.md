# Peer reviews and inspections

The peer reviews of requirements, plans, design, code and test procedures (%[SWE-087], %[SWE-088], %[SWE-089]), after handbook topic 5.03. Skein records reviews of links (`skein review`) and approvals (`skein approve`); this record holds each review's data.

## Each review

The item reviewed and the kind of review, the date, the time spent (in all, and in preparation), the participants and their areas, the defects found (major and minor, by type), and the result: passed, or to be reviewed again.

## Checklists and criteria

The checklists used, and the entry and exit criteria of each kind of review.
